Kettle's first public test build for the KONKR Pocket FIT Elite (Snapdragon 8 Elite). It runs from a microSD card; Android on the internal storage is not touched.

Starting it from the card needs ROCKNIX's SM8750 ABL in place of the stock bootloader, installed from Android (the unit's bootloader comes unlocked, and its Android has a root helper). Back up the stock ABL first. How it boots: https://github.com/kettlelinux/kettlelinux/blob/main/docs/POCKETFITELITE.md

## What's in it
- Game Mode with Valve's arm64 Steam client, and a Plasma desktop
- The Plasma desktop inside Game Mode as well, from Desktop in Steam's library
- Atomic A/B updates from Steam's own Check for updates, with automatic fallback if an update fails to boot
- Kettle Installer: install to the internal storage next to Android, with a backup of the internal storage first
- Game Settings: everything set per game in one place, with tabs: FEX, DXVK, vkd3d, Turnip and Proton settings (shared with other players), power and refresh rate, upscaling and frame generation (off until enabled per game), and .NET games run natively on ARM64
- Power control from Steam's Performance panel: TDP, performance profiles, GPU clock and per-game fan curves, with Auto TDP on by default
- Epic Games, GOG, Amazon Games and Flathub's ARM64 apps in Game Mode, through Game Stores, and Amethyst Mod Manager
- Device Settings: battery charge limit and charge speed, lights, the screen's refresh rate, and system settings (start-up mode, SSH, factory reset)
- Crash reports in Quick Access, which you can share to report a crash on GitHub
- Android games in Steam, through Lepton, the Android layer Valve made for the Steam Frame
- Windows games outside Steam: Wine with FEX, DXVK and vkd3d-proton, plus Lutris and Heroic
- GE-Proton and Proton-CachyOS, and emulators and streaming apps from Flathub, installed from Kettle Welcome
- A microSD card you put in becomes a Steam library, as on a Steam Deck
- The 1080x1920 screen at 60, 90, 120 or 144 Hz
- The Adreno 830 with Turnip Vulkan: in The Witcher 3 at 720p, about 55 fps where an Odin 2 Portal gets 30
- The gamepad and system buttons as one Steam Deck controller in Steam
- Deep sleep: about 14 mA asleep

## Known issues
- Early build. Checked on a unit: starting from the SD card and from the internal storage, the screen and touch, the GPU, the controls, speakers, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, the fan, charging with the charge limit and charge speeds, sleep, and A/B updates.
- Wi-Fi now and then stops answering (after a boot or a wake); a restart brings it back.
- No gyro yet, and no rumble.
- Not tested yet: the stick lights, the headphone jack, DisplayPort out and its audio, and Desktop Mode's screen rotation.
