The first public test build for the AYN Thor. It runs from a microSD card; Android on the internal storage is not touched. It is the same system as the Odin 2 Portal's, set up for the Thor's two screens and controls.

## What's in it
- Game Mode with Valve's arm64 Steam client on the top screen, and a Plasma desktop across both screens
- The bottom screen in Game Mode: a touch shell of its own (Plasma Mobile), for a browser, a guide or a chat next to your game. Quick Access > Screens turns it off or on and sets its brightness.
- The AYN key opens Steam's Quick Access menu
- Atomic A/B updates from Steam's own Check for updates, with automatic fallback if an update fails to boot. Thor updates are separate from the Portal's.
- Firefox plays video with the hardware video decoder (H.264, HEVC, VP9 and AV1), YouTube included
- Frame generation and upscaling plugins in Game Mode, off until enabled per game
- Android games in Steam, through Lepton, the Android layer Valve made for the Steam Frame
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean text in games and the desktop

## Known issues
- The GPU can freeze. We're working on it.
- Early build: much of it has not been tested on a Thor yet. Tested: starting from the SD card through U-Boot, both screens and touchscreens, the speakers and microphone, Wi-Fi, the controls, suspend, and video in Firefox.
- The bottom screen in Game Mode is new. If it misbehaves, turn it off in Quick Access > Screens and please open an issue.
- Not tested yet: starting from the ROCKNIX ABL, the Kettle Installer (internal storage), headphones, the lid, and power limits on battery (the Thor uses the Portal's for now).
- The microSD card runs in its slower mode (about 13 MB/s), so games load more slowly from it than on the Portal.
- Android games: Android's status and navigation bars show, and can cover buttons in some games. Games that need Google Play services or buy things through Google Play inside the game don't work, and neither do games made only for x86 or for Android 12 and newer.
