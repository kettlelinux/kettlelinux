Game settings

Settings that got games running on Kettle Linux, shared by players after playing with them. Verified entries were checked by the Kettle team; community ones show how many players they worked for.

1. Try settings

In Game Mode, open Quick Access > Game Settings and pick the game. Change FEX, graphics or Proton options, or try a preset, until it runs.

2. Use what works

Known good settings lists this database's entries for the game: Apply one, play, and say whether it worked for you too.

3. Share yours

Played 5 minutes with your settings and it works? Mark it as working and Share. Only the settings, your device model and Kettle build are sent.
