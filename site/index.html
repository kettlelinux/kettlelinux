A look around Game Mode

Everything in Quick Access

Press … for Quick Access, then the plug icon: Kettle's tools sit right next to Steam's own. The same tour opens on a new device, in Welcome.

Game Settings

Every game, set up its own way

A tab for each part of a game. It opens on the game that's running, sets up any installed game before you start it, and All games holds the defaults. Everything is off until you turn it on.

Compat

How a Windows game runs: the Proton version and FEX, DXVK, vkd3d and Turnip options, presets for the engine it's built on, and known good settings other players shared in Kettle's game database.

Perf

A fan curve or fixed speed, CPU core and clock limits and the refresh rate, for one game or all. Auto TDP holds Steam's frame limit on the lowest clocks that keep it.

Upscale

FSR 1 through gamescope for any game, or Snapdragon GSR 2, Arm ASR and FSR 2.2 in place of a game's DLSS, FSR 2+ or XeSS, through OptiScaler.

Frame Gen

Kettle's own frame generation: 2×, 3× or the fewest frames that fill the display, with the game's base frame rate capped for you.

Extras

GE-Proton, Proton-CachyOS and AMD FSR 3.1, each downloaded from its own project and checked before it's installed.

Game Stores

Your other libraries, in Steam

Sign in once and your games install, update and land in your Steam library with Proton, sharing sign-ins and installs with Heroic on the desktop.

  • Epic Games
  • GOG
  • Amazon Games
  • Battle.net
  • Flathub
  • Android

Flathub's ARM64 apps by category, emulators and streaming apps among them; Android games from F-Droid or an APK file.

Device Settings

The device, looked after

The device's own settings, a tab each. A tab for something your device doesn't have, motion sensors or a second screen, isn't shown.

Power

A charge limit that keeps the battery from sitting at 100%, the charge speed, and the screen's refresh rate, from 50 to 165 Hz on the Portal.

Lights

The rings around the sticks, and the Portal's power button light: a color, the battery level, breathe or rainbow. Dark while the device sleeps.

Gyro

Motion controls for Steam Input, on while a game runs, idle otherwise to save power.

Screens

The Thor's bottom touch screen: on or off (off, it stays dark and ignores touches), its own brightness, and 30 Hz instead of 60 for a little less power.

System

Whether Kettle starts in Game Mode or the desktop, the SSH server, a reset of settings or everything, and bootloader updates.

AYN Thor

A second screen that earns its place

On the Thor, the bottom screen runs Kettle's Performance app next to Game Mode: live readings while you play, a trackpad and a keyboard for the top screen, and the device's settings within reach.

Stats

The game's frame rate, frame times and 1% low, with a graph of the last ten seconds; power draw against the TDP limit, battery, CPU and GPU clocks and load, temperatures, fan, memory and the performance profile. No overlay over the game.

Trackpad

The bottom screen as a mouse for the top one, over Steam and the game: one finger moves the pointer, a tap clicks, two fingers scroll or right click, and Left and Right buttons stay down while touched.

Keyboard

A full PC keyboard, Esc and F1–F12 to the arrow keys. Keys stay down while touched, so they work in games, and Shift, Ctrl and Alt latch for the next key.

Settings

Steam's performance profile, TDP and GPU clock, Auto TDP, fan and CPU for the running game or all games, both screens' refresh rates, the bottom screen's brightness, and charging. The same settings as Quick Access, so a change in one shows in the other.

Diagnostics

Everything about the device

Power draw, temperatures and clocks, live, and the chip, battery health, storage and network, from the bottom of Device Settings.

Also in Kettle

Built for handhelds

Atomic A/B updates

Signed images installed from Steam's own Check for updates, a read-only root, and automatic fallback if an update fails to boot.

Mainline kernel

Linux 7.2 with a pinned patch stack for the panel, gamepad, LEDs, haptics, microSD, Wi-Fi and suspend.

The desktop in Game Mode

Desktop in Steam's library opens the Plasma desktop inside Game Mode, with no switch to Desktop Mode.

Games outside Steam

ARM64EC Wine with FEX, DXVK and vkd3d-proton, plus Lutris and Heroic built for arm64.

Native ARM64 games

.NET games such as Terraria and Stardew Valley run their own code on ARM64 instead of under FEX: one switch in Game Settings.

Android games

Android games in Steam and Game Mode, through Lepton, the Android layer Valve made for the Steam Frame.

Crash reports

A report for every game or system crash, kept on the device. Share one with your details taken out, and report it from your phone.

Two screens on the Thor

Steam on the top screen and a touch shell on the bottom one in Game Mode, and both screens in the desktop.

Game database

Settings other players found working, per device, applied in one step from Game Settings: browse them.
