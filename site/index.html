A SteamOS-like Linux distro for the AYN Odin 2 Portal and AYN Thor.

Valve's arm64 Steam client, a handheld Game Mode, a Plasma desktop and atomic A/B updates, on a patched mainline kernel.

Each device has its own image: pick the one for yours. Write it to a 32 GB or larger microSD card. Booting it needs the ROCKNIX ABL or U-Boot; Android on internal storage is left alone. Follow the install guide. Once installed, updates come through Steam's own Check for updates.

What you get

SteamOS experience

Valve's arm64 Steam client with its handheld Game Mode, and switching between Game Mode and Desktop.

Atomic A/B updates

Signed images installed from Steam, a read-only root, and automatic fallback if an update fails to boot.

Mainline kernel

Linux 7.2 with a pinned patch stack for the panel, gamepad, LEDs, haptics, microSD, Wi-Fi and suspend.

Per-game extras

Frame generation, SGSR, Arm ASR and FSR 2 upscaling through Decky plugins, all off until you enable them for a game.

Power control

TDP, performance profiles, GPU clock and per-game fan curves from Steam's Performance panel.

Games outside Steam

ARM64EC Wine with FEX, DXVK and vkd3d-proton, plus Lutris and Heroic built for arm64.

Android games

Add an Android game to Steam and play it in Game Mode, through Lepton, the Android layer Valve made for the Steam Frame.

Two screens on the Thor

Steam on the top screen and a touch shell on the bottom one in Game Mode, and both screens in the desktop.
